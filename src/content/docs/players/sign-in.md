---
title: Your account
description: Why you need a NodeMP account, how to create one and sign in through your browser, playing without one in Test Drive, and bans.
---

You can play NodeMP with or without an account. With one, you have the same name on every server, and servers can check that it's really you. Without one, in Test Drive, NodeMP picks your name; some servers don't let guests in.

## Create an account

Create an account at [nodemp.com/register](https://nodemp.com/register). You need:

- a username: 3 to 24 characters, Latin letters, digits, hyphens and underscores;
- an e-mail address that isn't disposable: the confirmation link goes there;
- a password: 8 to 200 characters, not on lists of leaked or common passwords;
- two checkboxes: *I accept the Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy* and *I consent to the processing of my personal data*.

The account is created when you open the link in the e-mail. Hosts use the same account; there is no separate host account. If you forget your password, click **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page; the reset link arrives by e-mail and is valid for one hour.

## Sign in to the launcher

The launcher doesn't ask for your password: you sign in on the website, in your browser.

1. In the sign-in window, click **Sign in with browser**. The launcher shows a sign-in code and opens nodemp.com.
2. Sign in on the site if you haven't yet. With two-factor authentication on, the site asks for the code from your app.
3. Check that the code on the site matches the one in the launcher and click **Allow sign-in**. The launcher greets you and opens the main window.

The code is valid for 10 minutes; while it waits, the launcher shows the time left (*Waiting for approval · 9:59*). **Open again** reopens the page, **Copy link** copies the link and the code, and **Back** returns to the choice.

The launcher remembers your sign-in and next time opens the main window at once.

### If sign-in doesn't work

| What it says | What to do |
|---|---|
| *The browser did not open. Go to the address below and enter this code.* | The link and the code are already on your clipboard. Open the address in any browser and enter the code. |
| *The code expired. Get a new one and confirm in the browser.* | Click **Get a new code**. |
| *Sign-in was declined in the browser.* | Someone clicked **Deny** on the site. If it was you, click **Get a new code**. |
| *Browser sign-in did not work: …* | After the colon comes the reason or NodeMP's answer. If the connection breaks, the launcher retries up to four times. Click **Get a new code**. If NodeMP names a support address, a **Support** button appears too. |

## Test Drive

**Continue as Test Drive** lets you in without an account. A guest name looks like `Guest-7K3MQ` and stays the same for every join from the same launcher installation.

Each server decides whether to let guests in: see the **Test Drive** mark in the list and the **Access** row in the server's card ([Join a server](/players/join/#find-a-server)). If a guest tries a server that needs an account, NodeMP refuses and the launcher offers **Sign in**.

Test Drive isn't remembered: the sign-in window comes back at the next start. To sign in later, click **Settings → Account → Sign in**. Guests can't wait in a full server's queue ([When the server is full](/players/join/#when-the-server-is-full)).

## Your account in the launcher

**Settings → Account** shows your name and roles. **Sign out** switches the launcher to Test Drive. The **On the site** block links to your account pages in the browser: **Profile**, **Security**, **Connections**, **My servers**, **Session** and **Public page**.

If the moderators warn you, the launcher says so and shows the reason under **Warnings from the moderators**. The site shows the warning on your profile while it is in force.

## Bans

A banned account can't join NodeMP servers. The launcher shows the *Account banned* window at startup, when you sign in, or when you join a server. The window shows **Reason**, **Until** (or *No end date*) and the account number; with no details, it says *No reason or end date was given.*

**Close** closes the launcher. If NodeMP names a support address, a **Contact support** button appears next to it. A ban with an end date lifts by itself when the date passes.

A ban on a single server comes from its host and doesn't affect other servers. The server tells you when you join: `You are banned from this server`, or the reason the host wrote.
